TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

How RinkLens measures players.

Every score is labeled by its source, formula, and limitations.

01 / STATISTICAL LAYER

Production Index

With NHL summary data, position-relative percentiles combine points per 60 minutes (55%), shots per 60 (30%), and goals per 60 (15%). Players with fewer than ten games are excluded from the percentile reference sample when enough eligible players exist.

This is a simple production index, not an expected-goals model or an all-in-one measure of defensive impact.

02 / ADVANCED LAYER

5-on-5 Analytics Index

When a compatible MoneyPuck skater CSV is imported, the model uses five-on-five on-ice expected-goal share (45%), Corsi share (20%), individual expected goals per 60 (20%), and points per 60 (15%), rated within position groups.

Shares reflect on-ice outcomes, not isolated player causality. Usage, teammates, matchups and sample size all matter.

03 / OBSERVATION LAYER

Eye-Test Grade

A scout assigns a one-to-five grade to skating, puck skills, hockey IQ, defensive play, compete level, and transition. The mean is multiplied by twenty to create a score out of 100.

Unscouted players receive no eye-test score. Notes remain local unless you connect a Supabase account and sign in. Account data is private under database row-level security.

04 / COMBINED VIEW

Blended Evaluation

For players with a completed scouting report, blend the statistical index and scouting grade. The default is 60% data and 40% eye test. You can adjust the weighting in the comparison view.

Scores are experimental scouting aids; they are not official ratings, independently validated player projections, or substitutes for contextual film review.

Data and attribution

NHL statistics: Publicly accessible NHL statistics endpoints; this project is independent and not endorsed by the NHL.

Advanced statistics: MoneyPuck CSV import. Credit MoneyPuck.com when using its data. MoneyPuck publishes separate terms for noncommercial and other uses, so verify permissions before using it in a commercial site.

Auto Scout Estimate: An automatically calculated, position-relative 0–100 statistical proxy: xGF% 25%, individual xG/60 30%, CF% 12%, FF% 8%, high-danger chance share 10%, points/60 15%. Missing metrics are excluded and remaining weights normalized. This is not an evaluation of video or a substitute for an actual eye test; it is excluded from the manual combined score to avoid double-counting data.

Source separation: Loaded statistics, manually entered scouting grades, and calculated scores are identified separately throughout the interface.
