ONE TRANSPARENT MONEYPUCK-BASED SCORE

How the Advanced Stats Score works

A simple 0–100 summary of five MoneyPuck 5-on-5 advanced statistics plus five-on-five games played. Forwards and defensemen use different weights and are compared only with their position group within the selected season. Scores require real five-on-five minutes and games. This is a descriptive RinkLens calculation, not a score provided by MoneyPuck.

MoneyPuck 5v5 metricForwardsDefensemen
Expected goals share (xGF%)35%40%
Individual expected goals / 60 (ixG/60)25%10%
Shot-attempt share (CF%)10%15%
Unblocked shot-attempt share (FF%)5%10%
High-danger shot share (HDCF%)15%15%
Five-on-five games played (GP)10%10%
Total100%100%

Why different weights? Forwards get more emphasis on individual expected-goal creation; defensemen get more emphasis on on-ice chance quality and shot shares. Games played carries 10% for both positions to account for availability. CF% and FF% overlap, so their combined weight is limited. These are transparent editorial choices—not a validated all-around player-value model.

Eligibility: A player must have at least 10 five-on-five games and 100 minutes of five-on-five ice time. Early in the season (until the most games played by any skater reaches 15), the requirements are 3 five-on-five games and 30 five-on-five minutes. At least 30 qualified players at the same position group must have data for each included metric.

Calculation: Take the same-position, same-season percentile of each advanced metric and five-on-five GP, apply the corresponding weights, and average. Games played is a separate 10% component and cannot replace the requirement for four actual advanced metrics, including xGF% and ixG/60. If one optional metric is missing, rescale the remaining weights rather than treating it as zero. The result is adjusted toward 50 using effective five-on-five games / (effective games + 10); effective games is the lesser of actual five-on-five games and five-on-five minutes / 12. This reliability adjustment is separate from GP’s explicit 10% weight, so games played influences both durability and sample-size reliability.

Reliability labels: Small sample: fewer than 20 five-on-five games or 300 five-on-five minutes. Moderate sample: fewer than 45 five-on-five games or 750 minutes. Larger sample: at least 45 games and 750 minutes. Labels describe sample size, not certainty that a player is good or bad.

Advanced Stats Score uses MoneyPuck 5v5 data only. Eye Test is a separate community opinion. Scores are never combined. A dash means that required evidence is unavailable, not a zero rating.

STARTING FORMULA + REAL VIEWER GRADES

Eye Test

Every eligible skater gets a Formula Baseline calculated from MoneyPuck season games played, average ice time, and points per game. It is an estimate, not an actual Eye Test observation. Signed-in viewers can grade eight position-specific skills from 1–10. Their grades influence the combined score; individual reviews affect only the reviewer's private preview until three community reviewers qualify. The original Advanced Stats Score stays separate.

How the starting score works

These are the weights from the other NHL Player Ranking project, applied to this season's skaters using same-position percentiles. The original 100-career-game minimum is not applicable to a single season. Goalies are not included because this version of RinkLens contains MoneyPuck skaters only.

First, convert each input to its position-group percentile. Next, average using the stated weights, then draw early-season scores toward 50 with GP / (GP + 10). The formula creates a statistical estimate, not observations of skating, decision-making or defensive skill.

How community grading changes it

Before reviews: combined score = formula baseline. After your first grade: a private preview uses 70% baseline / 30% your score. After three public reviewers: the public combined score uses 50% baseline / 50% community average. At four reviewers the human contribution is 60%, and at five or more it is 70%. The community average is always displayed separately from the baseline so viewers can see what actually came from people.

Current public community averages require three distinct reviewers. A single signed-in person's rating is displayed to that person privately, not publicly. More reviewers gradually give real fan observation more influence. This blended Eye Test is distinct from the MoneyPuck Advanced Stats Score.

STAT DEFINITIONS

What do these numbers mean?

All-situations counting totals and 5-on-5 advanced metrics are kept separate.

Points / 60 (P/60)

Goals plus assists for every 60 minutes of the player's total ice time. All game situations.

Expected goals share (xGF%)

Share of expected goals generated by the team while the player is on the ice at 5-on-5. This reflects chance quality and is influenced by teammates and opponents.

Corsi (CF%)

Share of all shot attempts by the player's team while on the ice at 5-on-5. It does not directly measure time of possession.

Fenwick (FF%)

Share of unblocked shot attempts at 5-on-5.

Individual expected goals / 60 (ixG/60)

Expected goals generated by the player's own shots per 60 minutes of 5-on-5 ice time.

High-danger chance share (HDCF%)

Share of high-danger shots for versus against while the player is on ice at 5-on-5.

The Advanced Stats Score and Formula Baseline are separate RinkLens calculations derived from MoneyPuck data, not official MoneyPuck ratings or subjective scout grades. The combined Eye Test is a clearly identified blend of the baseline and actual viewer grades when available. Small season samples may be volatile. A dash means required data is unavailable, not a zero rating. See MoneyPuck's glossary.
