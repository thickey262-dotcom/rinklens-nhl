How the Advanced Stats Score works

A simple 0–100 summary of five separate MoneyPuck 5-on-5 statistics. We compare forwards with forwards and defensemen with defensemen within the selected season. This is a descriptive RinkLens calculation, not a score provided by MoneyPuck.

Expected goals share (xGF%)35%
Individual expected goals per 60 (ixG/60)25%
Shot-attempt share (CF%)15%
High-danger shot share (HDCF%)15%
Unblocked shot-attempt share (FF%)10%

Calculation: Convert each available stat into a position-group percentile, multiply by its weight, add the results, then adjust the total toward 50 when the player has played relatively few games. A player needs at least four of the five metrics, including xGF% and ixG/60; available weights are rescaled when one other metric is missing. At least 30 peers with a given metric are required.

Sample-size rule: At least 10 games, reduced to 3 while the season's top games-played total is below 15. Early scores are more conservative because the sample is small. A dash (—) means insufficient information, not a bad rating.

Advanced Stats Score is statistical and automatic. Eye Test is based on separate viewers' opinions. They are never combined. Neither score is an official league talent rating.

STAT DEFINITIONS

What do these numbers mean?

All-situations counting totals and 5-on-5 advanced metrics are kept separate.

Points / 60 (P/60)

Goals plus assists for every 60 minutes of the player's total ice time. All game situations.

Expected goals share (xGF%)

Share of expected goals generated by the team while the player is on the ice at 5-on-5. This reflects chance quality and is influenced by teammates and opponents.

Corsi (CF%)

Share of all shot attempts by the player's team while on the ice at 5-on-5. It does not directly measure time of possession.

Fenwick (FF%)

Share of unblocked shot attempts at 5-on-5.

Individual expected goals / 60 (ixG/60)

Expected goals generated by the player's own shots per 60 minutes of 5-on-5 ice time.

High-danger chance share (HDCF%)

Share of high-danger shots for versus against while the player is on ice at 5-on-5.

The Advanced Stats Score is a custom RinkLens formula derived from MoneyPuck data, not a MoneyPuck-published player rating. Advanced metrics describe events and chance quality, not a subjective scout grade. Small samples may be volatile. A dash means no value was supplied or an Eye Test score does not yet qualify; it never means a zero rating. See MoneyPuck's glossary.
